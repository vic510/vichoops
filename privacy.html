Privacy Policy – Vic Hoops Publisher

Last updated: October 28, 2026

Vic Hoops Publisher ("the app") is a private tool operated by Vic Hoops. It uploads videos made by Vic Hoops to the Vic Hoops YouTube channels (@vichoops and @vichoopstw). It is not offered to the public.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using the app you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. The Google Privacy Policy also applies.

What data the app accesses

  • Permission to upload videos to the authorized YouTube channel (youtube.upload).
  • Read-only access to the authorized channel's basic information, used to confirm the upload goes to the right channel (youtube.readonly).

The app does not access viewers' data, comments, email, contacts or any other Google account data.

How data is stored and used

OAuth tokens are stored only on our own computer, with access limited to the operator, and are used only to upload Vic Hoops videos. We do not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties, and we do not use it for advertising.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time on the Google Account third-party connections page. When access is revoked, we delete the stored tokens. To request deletion, email us.
